# Nalumi

Chat with your own AI. Your server, your data — nothing leaves your device.

Nalumi is an Android chat client for self-hosted LLM servers: Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, llama.cpp, LiteLLM, and other OpenAI-compatible backends. Your conversations stay on your device and go only to the server you configure.
